WHY VERTO SKILLX EXISTS

Knowledge grows when we share it.

Verto SkillX is a peer-to-peer learning and teaching marketplace designed around the skills already present in a student community.

The problem

Students often want to learn practical skills but may not know whom to ask, where to begin, or how to find affordable, approachable guidance. At the same time, other students already have useful skills but have no simple way to share them.

Our solution

Verto SkillX brings learners and student mentors together in one place. Students can list skills, choose whether to learn or teach, share availability, send connection requests and communicate after connecting.

How it works

  1. Create a student profile.
  2. Explore skills such as Python, web development, photography or Excel.
  3. Choose “I want to learn” or “I can teach”.
  4. Add your available day and time.
  5. Find a matching student and send a request.

Our vision

Make learning more collaborative, practical and accessible. A student who is a beginner in one area can be a mentor in another. Everyone has something to learn and something to share.
