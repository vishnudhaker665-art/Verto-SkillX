Frequently asked questions

Some useful answers about how the Verto SkillX demo works.

What is Verto SkillX?

A student-to-student platform where people can discover skills, find peer tutors and share knowledge.

Is it free to join?

This assignment prototype is free to use. It does not process payments.

Can I be both a learner and a teacher?

Yes. Select “Both” during registration and choose multiple skills.

How do I add my availability?

Open a skill card, choose learn or teach, then select a day and time on the schedule page.

How do requests and chat work?

Send a request from the student directory. The other profile can accept it. The prototype stores requests and sample messages in this browser only.

Will my profile be visible to everyone?

In this front-end demo, profiles are stored in the browser's local storage. A production version needs a secure backend, access controls and privacy policy.
